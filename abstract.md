We define optimization problems, $r$-approximation guarantees, independent
running-time guarantees, and L-reductions. PTAS, FPTAS, EPTAS, and QPTAS
combine correctness and time bounds on the same explicit program, with
ratio $1+\varepsilon$ for every real $\varepsilon>0$.
Instances and solutions are typed mathematical objects with fixed
representations. Programs use the archive's word-RAM semantics and may be
compiled from high-level implementations. Resource bounds control
instruction count and sufficient word width. This submission contains
definitions only.
